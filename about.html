Himalayas Digital

Himalayas Digital is a classified ads listing, content, conversation, community building and commerce platform.
Himalayas Digital platform's goal is to empower life, living, livelihood, and lifestyle of people living in the Himalayas region by bringing access to information via classified ads, events, stories, business listing, TV – all on a single platform with the focus on the Himalayan region.

Himalayas Digital platform has been created to bring together providers and seekers. Providers and seekers of ideas, inspiration, products, services, solutions, jobs, careers and mentorship. Himalayas Digital platform's goal is to serve as a classified ads platform that is available for free for all it's users. In the future, providers may promote their ads for a fees.
